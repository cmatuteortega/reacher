Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Contacto is an app that reminds you to call the people you care about. It is built so your data stays on your phone: there are no accounts and no ads, and the people you add, their numbers and your notes never leave it. The only things the app sends are anonymous crash reports, which you can turn off, and anonymous usage statistics, only if you agree to them.

What data it keeps

Only on your phone, in the app's private database:

  • For each person you add: name, phone number, how often you want to talk to them, when you last did, their birthday (if you enter it or it is in your contacts), notes, circle and your preferred way to reach them.
  • Your settings: reminder hours, theme, view and other preferences.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Crash reports and usage statistics

These are the only things that leave your phone, and neither of them includes the names, numbers, notes, birthdays or circles you keep in the app.

  • Crash reports (on by default; turn them off in Settings › Privacy). If the app crashes or freezes, it sends a technical report to Sentry so the problem can be fixed: what failed in the code, the app version, the phone model, the Android version, a random installation identifier and the app's recent steps (such as which screen was open). Screenshots and the content of the screen are never included.
  • Usage statistics (off unless you agree, when the app asks or in Settings › Privacy). Anonymous counts of what gets used, sent to PostHog on servers in the European Union: for example that a person was added (with how often you want to talk to them, not who), that a reminder was snoozed, or which screen was opened, along with the app version, phone model, Android version, language and a random identifier. No user profile is created. If you turn statistics off, the random identifier is discarded.

Both services receive the data over an encrypted connection and are set not to store IP addresses. Sentry deletes crash reports after 90 days at most, and PostHog keeps statistics for up to one year. We do not sell this data or share it with anyone else.

Permissions

  • Contacts (optional): to show the photo and read the birthday of people you have already chosen. Choosing someone from your contacts does not need it: the system picker only gives the app the one contact you pick.
  • Notifications: for the reminders.
  • Phone calls (optional): only if you choose “Call directly” as the way to reach someone.
  • Vibration, run at startup and background work: for haptics and so reminders keep working after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: only for the crash reports and, if you agree, the usage statistics described above.

Other apps and services

When you tap a reminder, Contacto opens the app you chose (phone, SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram) with that person's number. From there, that app's privacy policy applies. If you send feedback, your email app opens with a message you decide whether to send. The in-app rating prompt is handled by Google Play.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on, the system may include your people and the app's settings in the encrypted backup of your Google account, as with any other app. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

You can also export a backup file from Settings › Backup. It is saved wherever you choose and contains the names, numbers, notes, birthdays and settings you have in the app. Contacto never sends it anywhere; keep it somewhere private.

Deleting your data

You can remove anyone from their screen in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in system settings deletes everything. Your phone's contacts are never modified.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
