You are the sun, and the people you care about are your system.

Contacto reminds you to call, text or wish a happy birthday to each person as often as you choose. No accounts, no ads: your people stay on your phone.

• Pick someone from your contacts and say how often you want to talk: every week, every month, whatever suits you.
• One reminder a day, within your hours, with whoever is due. One tap opens a call, SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram, whichever you prefer for that person.
• Not now? “Later” snoozes it; pause someone for a few weeks if you need to.
• Bubbles with faces, orbits around the sun or a plain list: whoever is most due is closest and biggest.
• Birthdays: from your contacts or entered by hand, with a reminder to send your wishes.
• Circles and notes for each person.
• “Due today” widget and a shortcut to add someone.
• Light and dark themes, tablets and foldables.

Truly private: names, numbers and notes never leave your phone. The app only sends anonymous crash reports (you can turn them off) and, if you agree, anonymous usage statistics. Your contacts are never modified.
